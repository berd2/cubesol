🎮 Cube Controls Guide

  • Rotate Layer: Drag any cubie (1-9) in the desired direction.
  • All Cubies (1-9): Free to rotate in both vertical and horizontal directions (4-way moves).
  • Middle Layers: Dragging a center (5) or edge (2,4,6,8) across its axis rotates the middle layers (M, E, S).
  • Rotate Camera: Drag the empty background to adjust each view individually.
  • Special Controls:
    • ⚡: Scramble the cube randomly.
    • ↪️: Undo the last manual move.
    • 🔴: Stop the auto-solver instantly.
    • ☰: Open the menu to reset camera angles, toggle single/dual view, save results, or adjust rotation speed.
